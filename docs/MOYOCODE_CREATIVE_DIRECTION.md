# MoyoCode Creative Direction

**Status:** proposed on 15 Nov 2026, awaiting the founder's approval. Full options are in `CREATIVE_DIRECTION_PROPOSALS.md`.

## In one line

**The Cut:** the diagonal from the MoyoCode M crosses out problems across the whole site, so every page shows the brand's promise, problem → fixed, as a single gesture.

## Feel

- Premium without being pretentious: black, one gold, sharp corners.
- Bold where it sells (hero, problems), calm where it builds trust (founder, work, contact).
- The visitor does something early: picks a problem, or ticks "That's me".

## What MoyoCode is not on this site

- Not a template agency: no stock photos, icon grids or "solutions" wording.
- Not an AI hype company: AI appears only as a tool inside a fix.
- Not bigger than it is: no invented clients, numbers or awards.
