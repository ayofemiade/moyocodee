# UX and Conversion Audit

15 Nov 2026. Measured against the brief's journey: recognition → clarity → trust → confidence → action.

| Stage | Today | Gap | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recognition:** "They understand my problem" | Strong hero and five problem quotes | Reading only; the visitor never confirms *their* problem | Problem chips in the hero; "That's me" toggles in Sound familiar? |
| **Clarity:** "I see how they could help" | Three service cards and pages | Services are described, never shown working | Each service gets a demonstration: before/after slider, illustrated dashboard, live enquiry timeline |
| **Trust:** "They can execute" | Founder quote only | No photo, no work, no named person behind the company, no contact details | Real founder photo and short bio; real case studies with permission; About page |
| **Confidence:** "I know what happens next" | One line promising a reply within one working day | The steps after submitting aren't explained, and there's no pricing signal | "What happens next" with 3 steps beside the form; optional "Projects typically start from…" line *(founder decision)* |
| **Action:** "I want to talk" | Form only, and it isn't connected | No WhatsApp, email or phone. The form can't deliver | Connect the form; add WhatsApp and email; carry chip and toggle choices into the form |

## Mobile enquiry journey

- **No menu below 768 px:** services are reachable only by scrolling. *Fix: a full-screen menu.*
- **Long form:** it sits at the very bottom of the page. *Fix: a sticky "Tell us your problem" bar on phones after the hero, plus a WhatsApp option.*

## Rules kept

- No fake testimonials, logos, statistics or delivery timelines.
- Illustrations are labelled as illustrations.
- "Within a minute" claims appear only after the instant-reply system is live and tested.
