# Creative Direction Proposals

15 Nov 2026. The founder chose Crossed Out (concept B) and then supplied a logo, a gold M built from two angled pieces. These proposals build on both. The brief's three exploration lenses (editorial, immersive, minimalist premium) each appear as one direction.

**Recommendation: Direction 1, "The Cut",** with one immersive moment borrowed from Direction 2 and Direction 3's restraint in the trust sections.

---

## Direction 1: The Cut (recommended)

**Idea:** the diagonal cut in the MoyoCode M *is* the strike-through. One angled gold line runs through the whole site. It crosses out problems, divides sections and points to the next step. Logo, video series and website become one gesture.

- **Hero (interactive):**
    - Three problems appear and are cut by a diagonal stroke at the logo's angle. Then "Tell us the problem. We'll build what solves it." lands.
    - Below it is a row of problem chips: *Slow replies · Chasing payments · Outdated website · Manual admin*. Tapping one cuts it, shows the matching fix in one line, and carries it into the enquiry form.
    - The visitor does something in the first five seconds, and that action starts the sale.
- **Sound familiar? (a conversation, not a list):**
    - Owner frustrations appear as short speech bubbles. These are neutral bubbles, not an imitation of WhatsApp or any other app.
    - Each has a "That's me" toggle. Pick two, and the section answers: "2 problems. Both fixable." with a button that brings them into the form.
- **Three ways we fix it, each with its own treatment:**
    - **Websites:** a before/after slider, using real work with permission.
    - **Software:** a small dashboard drawn in code, clearly labelled *Illustration*.
    - **Automation:** an enquiry travelling through reply, alert and follow-up as a live animated timeline.
- **How we work (the one immersive moment, from Direction 2):** a pinned scroll where the gold line draws down through Understand, Diagnose, Build and Improve. It doesn't hijack the scroll; it just progresses with it.
- **Work:** real case studies only, in Problem → What we built → What changed.
- **Founder and contact (Direction 3's restraint):** calm, lots of space, a real photo, plain words, direct WhatsApp and email.
- **Section transitions:** diagonal clip-path edges at the logo's angle, used sparingly.

**Why it wins:** it keeps what the founder loves, makes the logo meaningful, and turns two sections into interactions that feed the form. It also stays light: CSS for the hero, plus GSAP lazy-loaded for scroll.

**Risk:** angled cuts can feel aggressive if overused. Rule: at most one diagonal per screen.

---

## Direction 2: Problem → Fixed (immersive)

**Idea:** the homepage is one scroll story. It's 11:40 pm, an enquiry arrives at a business, and nobody answers. Then MoyoCode's fix assembles piece by piece: the site, the instant reply, the alert, the follow-up.

- **Strength:** the most memorable option, and it demonstrates the product thinking.
- **Costs:**
    - Heavy pinning and scroll choreography means more JavaScript and more mobile risk.
    - It tells one story, so visitors with a different problem may not see themselves.
    - Slower to build.
- **Verdict:** use its technique once, in "How we work" (Direction 1), not as the whole site.

---

## Direction 3: Gold Standard (minimalist premium consultancy)

**Idea:** quiet and expensive. Mostly black, the brushed-gold M as the only image, large calm type, almost no motion. It reads like a top-tier consultancy.

- **Strength:** builds the most trust for high-ticket buyers, and it's the fastest to load and build.
- **Costs:**
    - It drops the Crossed Out energy the founder chose.
    - It's less memorable and disconnected from the video series.
- **Verdict:** borrow its restraint for the founder, work and contact sections.

## Comparison

| | 1 · The Cut | 2 · Problem → Fixed | 3 · Gold Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Memorability | High | Highest | Medium |
| Trust for ₦1.35M / $2,000 buyers | High | Medium | Highest |
| Fit with the founder's choice and the videos | Highest | Medium | Low |
| Mobile performance risk | Low | High | Lowest |
| Build time | Medium | Highest | Lowest |
| Moves visitors into the form | Highest (chips and toggles) | Medium | Medium |

## Decisions the founder needs to make

1. **Direction:** 1 as recommended, or another.
2. **Logo:** supply the vector file, or approve a redrawn SVG.
3. **Text font:** Schibsted Grotesk (recommended) instead of Inter. See the design system.
4. **Hero headline:** visible from the start, with only the strikes animated (better LCP), or keep the 1-second delay.
5. **Installing UI UX Pro Max:** yes or no.
