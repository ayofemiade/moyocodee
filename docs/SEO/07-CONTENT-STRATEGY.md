# 07 · Content Strategy

**The principle (Google, "people-first content"):** only publish what MoyoCode can say from real experience, that helps a business owner decide something. No posts written to fill a keyword, and no rewrites of the top five results.

## Service pages first (done in this round, copy pending approval)

Each service page now has:
- the problem (approved copy)
- the fix (approved copy)
- **who it's for**
- **what we build** (concrete deliverables)
- **how a project runs**
- **questions people ask**

This is the content Google needs to understand the page, and the questions buyers ask before enquiring. New copy is marked `// pending` in `src/content/services.ts` and goes live only after the founder approves it at "push".

**Rules:**
- Every guide has the founder's first-hand detail (a real quote range, a real build, a screenshot) and a byline.
- No invented statistics. Third-party numbers are cited and dated.
- Each guide links to its service page, and the service page links back.
