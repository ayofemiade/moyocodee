**Method:** web search results for the core commercial queries. The available search tool is US-located, so it is **not a Nigerian SERP**; positions are indicative only. Google autocomplete was queried with `gl=ng`.

**No backlink or traffic data** (no paid tool access).

## What they do well

- **Directories win commercial "company in Lagos/Nigeria" queries.** A free, honest Clutch, DesignRush or TechBehemoths profile is the fastest route into those results. It's a listing, not a ranking.
- **Price-guide content captures the high-volume informational query** ("how much does a website cost in Nigeria").

## Where they are weak

- **Price guides are vendor-biased and repetitive:** Kolonell alone has 8+ near-identical "cost" posts. Few explain *why* a price changes in terms a business owner can use.
- **Agencies sell deliverables** ("corporate website", "eCommerce"), not problems. Almost none speak to "enquiries go unanswered", "spreadsheets that don't talk" or "chasing invoices", which is MoyoCode's positioning.
- **AI automation is served mostly by freelance gigs.** No credible Nigerian agency page explains *what to automate and what not to*. That's an open lane.

## Realistic opportunities

1. Directory profiles, honest and complete (Priority 3, founder action).
2. A problem-led service page per service: the problem, what we build, who it's for, how it runs, and FAQs.
3. One genuinely useful cost guide written from first-hand quoting experience (needs the founder's real input; see 07).
4. Own "AI automation for Nigerian businesses, explained plainly". Few credible pages compete.
