# 11 · Authority and Digital PR

**Rule:** earn it. No bought links, link schemes, PBNs, mass directory submissions or fake listings ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)).

| Opportunity | Why it's relevant | Action | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Client credit links** | The Segun Michael site already credits "Site by MoyoCode Technologies" as plain text | With the client's permission, make it a link to moyocode.com. Natural and relevant | Low |
| **Clutch, DesignRush, TechBehemoths, GoodFirms** | They rank for "company in Lagos/Nigeria" queries (02) | Honest profiles; ask real clients for verified reviews | Low–medium |
| **LinkedIn company page + founder profile** | Entity consistency; the founder already has an audience | Same name, logo and URL. Founder posts link to guides | Low |
| **GitHub organisation** | A developer-credibility signal | Link to moyocode.com from the org profile | Low |
| **Nigerian tech press** (TechCabal, Techpoint Africa, BusinessDay SME, Benjamindada) | Founder story: a student building a problem-first tech company publicly | Pitch only with a real hook (a launched product, an original data point, a notable project) | Medium |
| **Communities** (GDG Lagos, local startup meetups, university tech societies) | Real local relationships and citations | Talks and demos (e.g. "automating WhatsApp enquiries") | Medium |
| **Hackathons** (e.g. Ogun Digital Summit) | Event pages list participants and winners | Make sure the project page credits MoyoCode | Low |
| **Original resource** | Guide 1 with real quoted price bands is citable | Publish, then share with communities that ask "how much should a website cost?" | Medium |

**Measure:** referring domains and branded searches, both from Search Console. Don't use raw link counts.
