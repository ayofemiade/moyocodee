# 04 · Keyword Mapping

One primary cluster per URL, to prevent cannibalisation. Titles stay under about 60 characters and descriptions under about 155.

| URL | Primary target | Secondary | Title | H1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | MoyoCode Technologies (brand) + "website design, custom software and AI automation" (Nigeria) | problem-solving technology company | Website Design, Custom Software & AI Automation \| MoyoCode | Tell us the problem. We'll build what solves it. (brand line kept) |
| `/services` | services hub | — | Services: Websites, Software & Automation \| MoyoCode | Three ways we fix it |
| `/services/website-design` | website design company in Nigeria | business website design · website redesign · website for small business | Website Design & Development for Businesses \| MoyoCode | Website design and development: Be the obvious choice |
| `/services/custom-software` | custom software development company (Nigeria) | business management software · client portal · internal tools · custom web application | Custom Software Development for Businesses \| MoyoCode | Custom software development: Work the way your business actually works |
| `/services/ai-automation` | AI automation agency (Nigeria) | business process automation · WhatsApp automation · AI chatbot / receptionist · automated follow-up | AI & Business Process Automation \| MoyoCode | AI and business automation: Stop doing it by hand |
| `/contact` | contact MoyoCode / free diagnosis | — | Contact MoyoCode: Tell Us the Problem | What's slowing your business down? |
| `/privacy` | — (trust page) | — | Privacy Notice \| MoyoCode | Privacy notice |

**Rule:** the homepage never targets a single service phrase. It links to each service page with descriptive anchor text ("Website design and development"), and those pages own the phrases.

**Old URLs:** `/services/websites`, `/services/software` and `/services/automation` 308 to the new slugs. They are one day old; changing them now costs nothing.
