# 10 · International SEO Strategy

**Decision: one English site, one domain, no hreflang, no country pages.**

- **No hreflang:** there are no separate language or regional versions, so hreflang would be wrong.
- **No country pages:** UK and US pages would repeat the same services with a different country name. That's doorway content, and the research (03) shows those generic queries are saturated and local-intent.
- **Prices:** none are published. When guides quote prices they must say which currency and market they refer to (₦ for Nigeria), so foreign readers aren't misled.
- **Reaching UK and US buyers:** through specific problem content (e.g. "AI receptionist for small business"), the founder's LinkedIn, directories and referrals from diaspora clients. Not through location targeting.
- **Revisit when** Search Console shows meaningful impressions from a country, or MoyoCode has a real local presence or clients there worth a dedicated, genuinely different page.
