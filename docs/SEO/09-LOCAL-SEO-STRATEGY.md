# 09 · Local SEO Strategy

## Google Business Profile: likely NOT eligible yet

Google's [Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) require the business to make **in-person contact with customers** during stated hours, either at its address or by travelling to them (a service-area business). Online-only businesses are not eligible.

**Decision needed from the founder:**
- **If** MoyoCode meets clients in person in a real area (e.g. it visits clients in a city), it can be a **service-area business**: the address is hidden but must be real for verification, and the area is the city it actually serves. Use the checklist below.
- **If not** (fully remote), don't create a profile. A virtual office or borrowed address breaks Google's rules and risks suspension.

**Profile checklist (only if eligible):**
- Exact legal or trading name "MoyoCode Technologies" (no keywords added)
- Category "Website designer" plus secondary "Software company"
- The real service area only
- Hours
- Business email and phone once published
- Website `https://www.moyocode.com`
- Services matching the three service pages
- The logo
- Real photos
- Ask real clients for reviews (never incentivised, never fake)

## Alternatives that work for a remote business

- **Directory profiles with real details:** Clutch, DesignRush, TechBehemoths and GoodFirms. Free listings exist; check each site's current terms.
- **A LinkedIn company page** with the same name, logo and URL.
- **Nigerian business and tech communities** (see 11) for genuine local citations.
- **Consistency (NAP-style):** the same name, website and contact details everywhere, from the moment they're published.
