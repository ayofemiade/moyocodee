## Phrases avoided on purpose

- **"... jobs", "... salary", "... internship", "... course":** wrong audience.
- **"... free", "... template", "... github":** DIY intent.
- **City pages (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt…):** no confirmed local presence. Thin duplicates violate Google's doorway-page policy.
