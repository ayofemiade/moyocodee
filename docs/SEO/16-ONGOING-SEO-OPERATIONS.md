# 16 · Ongoing SEO Operations

**Targets are set only after 4–8 weeks of Search Console data.** Today there is no baseline traffic data to target against (01).

| Cadence | Task | Tool | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Search Console: coverage errors, new queries, manual actions | Search Console | 10 min |
| Weekly | Check every enquiry email was answered within one working day (the promise on the site) | Gmail | 5 min |
| Monthly | Impressions, clicks, CTR and position by page and query; note queries with impressions but low CTR, and rewrite those titles and descriptions | Search Console | 30 min |
| Monthly | Count enquiries, qualified enquiries and organic-sourced (ask "how did you find us?" on the call) | Founder's sheet | 15 min |
| Monthly | Publish or refresh one piece (08) | — | — |
| Monthly | Lighthouse mobile and desktop on home + one service; CWV field data in Search Console | Lighthouse, Search Console | 15 min |
| Quarterly | Full technical re-audit (rerun the 01 checks); keyword and cluster review with real queries; competitor SERP check (02); authority progress (11) | All | 2 h |
| Quarterly | Re-check: privacy notice still matches what the site does | Code audit | 15 min |

**Report format (monthly, one page):** cash-relevant first (enquiries and qualified), then visibility (impressions, clicks, top queries), then health (errors, CWV), then the next three actions.
