# Competitive Visual Research

Visited 15 Nov 2026 in the built-in browser at 1280×760. I recorded what could be observed directly, and say where a site could not be observed.

| Site | What it does well | Lesson for MoyoCode | Don't copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| [basement.studio](https://basement.studio) | 3D scene hero. Monospace UI labels. A "Human / Machine" toggle gives the visitor something to do in the first second. Tagline is blunt and personal | Give the visitor an action in the hero, not just a reading task | WebGL scene: heavy, and slow to render even in our browser |
| [linear.app](https://linear.app) | Restrained dark palette. One clear sentence. The product itself is the hero image | Showing the thing working beats describing it. Restraint reads as confidence | Product screenshots: we have no product to show |
| [instrument.com](https://www.instrument.com) | A huge wordmark builds in letter by letter on load | Type alone can be the hero if the motion is choreographed | Blank screen while the wordmark builds |
| [obys.agency](https://obys.agency) | Custom typeface. Project names as the main navigation | A distinctive type voice is the cheapest way to be memorable | Loading screen before any content (our first capture was a black screen) |
| locomotive.ca | Not observed: the site served a bot check | — | — |

Screenshots: `docs/screenshots/reference-basement.jpg`, `reference-linear.jpg`.

## Patterns that recur among strong studio sites

1. **One idea, executed hard.** A single motif carries the whole site, rather than many effects.
2. **Type as image.** Big, characterful type stands in for stock photography.
3. **Interaction early.** Something responds to the visitor in the hero.
4. **Rhythm changes.** Dense sections alternate with open ones, and dark with a single accent colour.

## The trap for MoyoCode

Award-winning studio sites sell *to other designers*, and many trade speed and clarity for spectacle. MoyoCode sells to business owners on ordinary phones and networks. The research supports taking their **conviction** (one motif, characterful type, early interaction) while rejecting their **cost** (WebGL, loaders, scroll hijacking).
